Privacy Policy — Table Tennis Scoreboard

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Table Tennis Scoreboard app ("the App") collects, why, and how it is handled.

The App does not require you to create an account, and does not collect or store any personal profile information (name, email address, photo, etc.) about you. Any player/team names you type in during a match stay on your device only — they are never sent anywhere.

Information the App Collects

Advertising identifier and ad interaction data (Google AdMob). The App shows ads via Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising identifier and information about how you interact with ads, to serve and measure ads. If you have purchased "Remove Ads / Pro", AdMob is never invoked and no ad-related data is collected.

Advertising consent choice (Google User Messaging Platform / UMP). If you are located in the European Economic Area or the UK, the App shows Google's consent form before any ad is requested, per GDPR requirements. Your consent choice is stored (by Google's UMP SDK) and used to determine what kind of ads, if any, may be shown. You can review or change this choice at any time via Privacy options in the app's menu.

Purchase data (Google Play Billing). If you purchase "Remove Ads / Pro," that one-time, non-consumable purchase is processed entirely by Google Play Billing. The App only receives confirmation of whether you own the purchase — it does not receive or store your payment details (card number, billing address, etc.), which Google Play handles directly under its own privacy policy.

The App does not collect location data, contacts, photos, or any other device data beyond what's described above.

Why This Information Is Collected

  • Advertising data funds the free version of the App.
  • Consent data ensures ads are only personalized where you've agreed, per GDPR/UK law.
  • Purchase data lets the App unlock ad removal and match-export for users who bought "Remove Ads / Pro," and lets you restore that purchase on a new device.

Third-Party Services

This App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — part of AdMob, same policy as above; see also Google's Consent Management documentation.
  • Google Play Billing — https://policies.google.com/privacy (payment details are handled by Google Play, not by this App).

Your Choices

  • Privacy options (EEA/UK only): review or withdraw your advertising consent at any time from the app's menu.
  • Restore purchases: re-link a previous "Remove Ads / Pro" purchase to a new device via the app's menu.
  • You can also manage your device's advertising identifier directly in your Android or iOS system settings (e.g. "Delete advertising ID" / "Limit Ad Tracking").

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, contact us using the details below so we can address it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this same URL with an updated "Last updated" date.
